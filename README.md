## Overview

This project builds a high-frequency trading (HFT) platform that simultaneously predicts minute‑level stock price jumps using deep‑learning models from Paper 1 and assesses consumer credit default risk in real time, leveraging the comparative insights from Paper 2. By fusing LSTM‑based price predictors with a lightweight MLP credit model, the engine can adjust trade sizing based on the creditworthiness of counterparties, mitigating counterparty risk. The innovation lies in the joint optimization of two traditionally separate domains—market micro‑structure prediction and credit risk—within a single, low‑latency pipeline, enabling broker‑dealers to execute trades that are both profitable and credit‑safe.

## Features

- Broker‑dealer risk‑adjusted trade execution
- Regulatory compliance dashboards for counterparty exposure
- Dynamic hedging strategies in volatile markets
